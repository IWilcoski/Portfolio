Bugged and Sorted

Engine/Language: Unity, C#

Itch.io Page: Bugged and Sorted


My Role

I was the sole programmer in this project, handling drag and drop in a 3D space, implementing play states with warnings for near-death, sound effects and music, and various sprite animations.

Game Summary

Bugged and Sorted was my 2nd collegiate game jam project, and was produced over three days. In the game you're a new employee at the Department of Bug Drone Operations or D.B.D. Here you drag and drop bugs on conveyour belts into categories like flight, wheels, radar, and screens. Keep an eye out for defective bugs that need to be trashed!

This project had difficulties in group communication, as the initial meetings never had the whole group present. It was a valuable lesson in taking lead and forced me to crunch and adapt the game concept to fit the misaligned elements.
