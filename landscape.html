Landscape Project

Engine: Unreal

Project Summary

My final assignment in my Unreal engine class, this was an explorable mountain pass, with a tunnel, trees, rocks, and river below.

The biggest successes of this project were figuring out how to carve a hole out of the landscape I created, and blendable textures with their own footstep sounds.

The landscape's various materials were assigned default physics materials which allowed a raycast to distinguish between the different materials despite sharing the same root. Then a corresponding footstep sound would be pitchshifted for variation and played.
